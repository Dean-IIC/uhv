Introduction

What is Universal Human Values?

Universal Human Values (UHV) is an educational initiative that aims to develop a comprehensive understanding of what it means to be a complete human being. It goes beyond technical skills to cultivate ethical awareness, emotional intelligence, and a sense of responsibility toward oneself, family, society, and nature.

The UHV framework, developed as part of the AICTE mandate for engineering institutions, is built on the foundation of Right Understanding — understanding oneself deeply, and then extending that understanding to relationships and the larger world.

Values Harmony Self-awareness Society Nature

The Purpose of Education

"The overall purpose of education is to enable a human being to live a fulfilling life, in harmony with family, society as well as nature."

The UHV Framework

UHV is structured around understanding at four levels: Self (Individual), Family, Society (including Profession), and Nature/Existence — each level building upon the previous to create a holistic worldview.

For Engineering Students

For engineering students, UHV helps develop professional ethics, social responsibility, and the wisdom to apply technical knowledge for the benefit of humanity while maintaining harmony with the environment.

AICTE Potential Nodal Center for Universal Human Values

HKBK College of Engineering has been officially recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) as a Potential Nodal Center for Universal Human Values. This prestigious recognition acknowledges our sustained efforts in implementing the UHV curriculum, conducting FDPs for faculty, and organizing various activities to inculcate universal values among engineering students.

Structure

The UHV Framework

Universal Human Values education is structured across four interconnected levels of existence.

  • 🧘
    Level 1: Individual (Self)

    Understanding oneself as a combination of the physical body and the 'I' (consciousness). Recognizing needs, emotions, and natural acceptance within oneself.

  • 👨‍👩‍👦
    Level 2: Family

    Understanding values in relationships — trust, respect, affection, care, guidance — and how to nurture fulfilling bonds with family members.

  • 🏘️
    Level 3: Society

    Understanding the societal order, justice, equality, and one's professional responsibilities toward colleagues, the institution, and society at large.

  • 🌿
    Level 4: Nature / Existence

    Understanding co-existence with nature — the interconnectedness of all living and non-living systems, and our responsibility to maintain ecological balance.
