Blood Donation Camp

A significant humanitarian initiative organized by the UHV Cell and Equal Opportunity Cell (EOC) in association with the National Service Scheme (NSS), FICCI FLO, and the Indian Red Cross Society. The camp promoted voluntary blood donation among students and staff, encouraging values of compassion, responsibility, and social consciousness. Venue: ONESB Seminar Hall.

ONESB Seminar Hall Humanitarian NSS + EOC

3rd December 2025

International Day of Persons with Disabilities

The EOC and UHV Cell commemorated International Day of Persons with Disabilities with a sensitization program titled "Towards Accessible Campus: Sensitization Program on Disability Inclusion." The program focused on creating a campus that is technologically adaptive, socially sensitive, and barrier-free. Active participation from students, faculty, and distinguished dignitaries. Duration: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM.

10 AM – 1 PM Disability Inclusion EOC + UHV

Human Rights Day — Free Health Check-Up Camp

HKBK College observed Human Rights Day (marking the adoption of UDHR in 1948) by organizing a Free Health Check-Up Camp themed "Health, Dignity, and Well-being for All." Organized by the UHV Cell, NSS Unit, and EOC in collaboration with Indian Designs Exports Pvt. Ltd., Prince Opticals, Procuredental Clinic and Lab Gen. Venue: BPT Seminar Hall.

BPT Seminar Hall Health Camp Human Rights

22nd December 2025

Outreach Program — Connecting with Specially Abled Children

An outreach visit to Samarthanam Disability Centre, HSR Layout, Bengaluru, organized by the CSE Department, UHV Cell, Differently Abled Cell, and Equal Opportunity Cell.
