Nurturing Values at HKBK College of Engineering

The Universal Human Values (UHV) Cell at HKBK College of Engineering was established in 2021 as part of the AICTE mandate to infuse value-based education in technical institutions. We have been recognized as an AICTE Potential Nodal Center for Universal Human Values.

Our mission is to help students develop a comprehensive worldview — understanding themselves, their relationships, and their responsibilities toward society, nature, and existence.

Our Purpose

Why Universal Human Values?

Technical education must go hand in hand with values that make engineers responsible citizens and compassionate human beings.

🌱

Holistic Development

Beyond technical skills, UHV helps students develop emotional intelligence, ethical reasoning, and a balanced worldview.

🤝

Harmonious Relationships

Building understanding of values like trust, respect, and care for nurturing healthy relationships in family, workplace and society.

⚖️

Ethical Engineering

Cultivating professional ethics so that engineers use their skills for the benefit of humanity and in harmony with nature.

🌍

Social Responsibility

Encouraging students to connect with and contribute to their communities through outreach programs and service activities.
