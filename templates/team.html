Principal & Patron

Dr. Mohammed Riyaz Ahmed

Principal, HKBK College of Engineering, Bangalore

Under the visionary guidance of Dr. Mohammed Riyaz Ahmed, the UHV Cell was established in 2021 and grew to achieve recognition as an AICTE Potential Nodal Center. His leadership has been instrumental in institutionalizing Universal Human Values education at HKBK College of Engineering.

UHV Coordinator

👩‍🏫
UHV Cell Coordinator

Prof. Khallikkunaisa

Department of Computer Science & Engineering, HKBK College of Engineering

Prof. Khallikkunaisa serves as the dedicated UHV Cell Coordinator at HKBK College of Engineering. She has been instrumental in planning and conducting all UHV activities — from induction programs and expert talks to national-level FDPs. She has represented the institution in the AICTE UHV Potential Nodal Centre Meets and VTU UHV lecture programs. Her commitment to values-based education has been the driving force behind the UHV Cell's success.
